## Getting Started

Nether Launcher, an minecraft java launcher based on pojav and mj aka mojo launcher. You may see very similarities between Mj and Nether Launcher but **are also very different**.

## Minecraft Versions

Nether Launcher support almost **all minecraft java version**, even if you have older version launcher apk, as minecraft upload new code, you get new version, so never worry about updating the apk or app.

## Installing Modpacks

Nether Launcher have a special tab for modpack, you can **download modpack with just one click**, we have inbuilt modpack system where you can download without going to curseforge or modrinth.

### What modpack service we use

We use modrinth and curseforge to show you the modpack, our system for searching modpack is not now good but soon it will be best.
